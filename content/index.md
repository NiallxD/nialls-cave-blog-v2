# Welcome👋

Thanks for visiting my blog. Here you will find my musings on a range of topics. Some may be essays on obscure concepts I find interesting, others just discussing my opinions on a particular topic.

What you read here is mostly my own opinions with the odd dash of facts thrown in where appropriate. If there is anything here you think is factually incorrect, or if you wish to discuss my opinions, please email me niall@niallbell.com. I enjoy diving into the things I find interesting!

## How to use this blog

I don't have any structure to when I may publish my thoughts here, but when I do they are published with an index number so the most recently published thoughts will appear at the top on the left side - I'm yet to decide what happens after post 9999.

### Finding things

There are a few ways to find my thoughts; the search box can be used to find posts by keyword; clicking tags on any post will show all posts with those tags; and you can always just browse the list until something catches your eye.

### Thought maturity

I can't take credit for this idea, but I like it. I represent the maturity of my thoughts (insert innuendo here) using a three-tier system.

- New thoughts have the maturity level of a **Seedling**: 🌱
- Somewhat developed thoughts have a maturity level of a **Sapling**: 🌿
- Well developed thoughts has a maturity level of a **Conifer**: 🌲

I use this system to remind myself to revisit thoughts which I maybe haven't for a while, and to communicate to my readers to what extent I feel my thoughts are developed. So if you see a **Sapling** you know I'm still finding my way, but if you find a **Conifer** you know that I firmly believe in it. 

## Before you go

I must remind you that these words are the opinions of my own (no AI slop either), here you might find something you feel is true, but it is only for certain true through my own eyes.
