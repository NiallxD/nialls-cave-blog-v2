# Welcome👋

Thanks for visiting my blog. Here you will find my musings on a range of topics. Some may be essays on obscure concepts I find interesting, others just discussion my opinions on something topical.

What you read here is mostly my own opinions with the odd dash of facts thrown in where appropriate. If there is anything here you think is factually incorrect, or if you wish to discuss my opinions, please email me [niall@niallbell.com](mailto:niall@niallbell.com) I enjoy diving into the things I find interesting!

### How to use my website

I don't have any structure to when I may publish my thoughts here, but when I do they are published with an index number so the most recently published thoughts will appear at the top on the left side - I'm yet to decide what happens after post 9999.

There are a few ways to find my thoughts; the search box can be used to find posts by keyword; clicking tags on any post will show all posts with those tags; and you can always just browse the list until something catches your eye.

### Before you go

I must remind you that these words are the opinions of my own, here you might find something you feel is true, but it is only for certain true through my own eyes.
