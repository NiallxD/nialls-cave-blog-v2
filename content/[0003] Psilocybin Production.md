### Psilocybin Production

Psilocybin is a psychoactive compound produced by several species of fungi. The compound is known to occur in species of the *Psilocybe* and *Inocybe* genera.

The compound was first isolated from psilocybe fungi by Albert Hofmann in 1958.

### Convergent Evolution

We do not yet known why fungi produce psilocybin. Its use to the mushroom are not known. Yet, multiple species have evolved to produce this compound, and each uses a different molecular pathway for that production.

This would suggest that the compound is important for the fungi.
